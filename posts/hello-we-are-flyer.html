Hello. We're Flyer.

A name with two meanings, a paper plane that won't stay in its box, and three words we intend to live up to.

14 October 2026

The name

Flyer is a play on two words.

  1. A flyer is a printed sheet: a poster, a leaflet, the most ordinary piece of advertising there is.
  2. A flyer is also something that flies: a pilot, a plane, the paper plane that breaks through the walls of the "ordinary box".

We start where every campaign starts, on a flat sheet of paper, and then we fold it into something that leaves.

The logo

A paper plane breaking out of a box. The box still carries the fold lines of the sheet it was made from: the idea and its origin in one mark.

The tagline

Ideas that land.Three words under the logo. A plane that only flies is a stunt; one that lands somewhere is a campaign.

The claim

Off the sheet, on the street.What we do, in one line: take the idea off paper and put it where people actually live.
